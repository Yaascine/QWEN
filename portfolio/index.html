01 ABOUT

BS Electrical Engineering from the School of Electrical Engineering & Mechanical Sciences (SEEMS), NUST, Islamabad. Five years in between consulting desks, transition shops and a very loud HV lab.

My work sits at the intersection of power systems and embedded electronics: I can run a fault-current study for a 132/13 kV grid station in the morning and debug a buck-converter control loop on the scope in the afternoon. I care about documentation, standards compliance (IEC 60255 / IEEE), and hardware that does not smoke on first power-up.

Currently building solar + storage monitoring hardware and writing about protection coordination on my blog. Available for contract and full-time roles.
